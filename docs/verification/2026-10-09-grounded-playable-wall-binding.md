# Explicit grounded playable-wall binding

Base: `3d69d1deba085d2021dd136d9fe28fa3b27d973c` / src `b28aae801813b0752fb68c784b3fd21a5fbae403`.

A first-base observation can now explicitly bind an accepted venue policy to an original physical wall contact. This connects an existing standard category that was previously left in `surface_policy_pending`: an ordinary playable wall encountered after independently established grounded fair territory does not itself terminate play. The existing semantic reference is [ballpark physical contract §11.1](../superpowers/plans/2026-09-20-ballpark-builder-canonical-geometry-plan.md#111-wall-collision), under the [world-first rules authority](../game-design/05-world-first-live-ball-architecture.md). That reference plan supplies the wall meaning; implementation is authorized by the user's current broader nonvisual Venue request, and no Ballpark Builder work is included. Physical collision response still belongs to the existing physical owner.

The optional `first_base_race.action.venuePolicy` is inert accepted configuration inside the immutable execution Source. It identifies its policy version, original game/play/pitch/fixture/venue/base-field/world-model identity and revision, availability day, registered RuleProfile revision and explicitly supported surface IDs. It supplies no physical contacts, geometry, afterWorld, fair flag, catch or ruling. Its identity is local to that accepted execution Source, not a second global policy store.

The Native field-execution owner validates that configuration against its authenticated original prefix and model, reconstructs actual surface contacts from owned field snapshots, and archives their hashes, raw positions and original free-ball rebound cursors. Core discharges a wall blocker only for a singleton supported contact strictly after the original ground and fair decisions, with a nondegenerate normal, no persistent-contact marker and an authenticated resolved rebound at that exact physical event. Every retained raw observation of that event must satisfy those conditions. A carried or interrupted capture contact cannot obtain eligibility merely from having a normal. Earlier, simultaneous, unlisted, unresolved and non-defender contacts retain their existing blockers. No wall can become an implicit fair, out-of-play, home-run or award event.

The archived raw contact list covers the accepted field snapshots used for this bound. It is not a complete manifest of every interrupted-acquisition contact nested elsewhere in the prefix. Those events do not receive rebound eligibility from this adapter, and their existing uncertainty remains pending.

All original contact frames remain available to catch eligibility and downstream owners. The possession-confirmation wrapper still withholds a race when an unconfirmed capture could affect it. Legacy Sources with no venue policy retain their serialized output shape and behavior. Official on-field decisions and reviews remain separate from this correct-rule evidence, and this addition creates no PlayEnd, closure, canonical Match mutation or next-play right.
