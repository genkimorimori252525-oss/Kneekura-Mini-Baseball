## User-facing requirements captured

1. Pitch **ball presentation samples** are nominally **1.2x denser than the v18 ball pass** (27.5ms / 1.2 = 22.9167ms), while batted balls retain the v18 nominal 27.5ms pass and normal scene/actors keep 55ms. No simulation ticks, physical velocity or umpire decisions are accelerated. Actual refresh depends on device Hz.
2. Do not cut to the next scene while the fair batted ball is flying/rolling/being fielded. Continue until captured/secured/physics settled or play leaves the field, followed by a visible result tail. A spectator may deliberately tap to skip once viewing from the runner camera.
3. Overhead camera shows LF, CF, RF name tags, even for tiny/distant sprites; all *runner* tags are red (`#ff7777`) to visually distinguish the defense.
4. Batter cannot begin running during follow-through. Browser slice: ball contact localTick39; preserve batter stance/4-frame B1 follow-through through localTick54; enter runner presentation at localTick55 and begin actual actor movement at localTick56. Production Canonical implementation must derive these boundaries from accepted body/pose states, not hardcoded role outcomes.
5. Batter POV swivels toward the actual batted-ball world position after contact. As batter runs on a fair hit (or during an unresolved line-adjacent foul), camera eye moves to the batter's world position at approximately 92% of their body height and pans to continue observing the same ball. The line-adjacent foul can produce a provisional run; once foul is recognized, foul watching resumes.
6. Foul ball remains visible **at most 5.0s after contact**. A tap before 2.0s queues a cut on/after 2.0s; a tap after 2.0s immediately advances to the next pitch. The preview scene uses the actual foul-ball positions as long as available, then holds the final settled/out-of-play position; user opt-out is explicit.
7. All four original camera modes are retained and a separate runner-view button is added. No replacement of v16 approved PNG/B1 player masks or font.

## Implementation

The user's downloadable v19 standalone HTML and source/test ZIP are generated by the local `build_v19.py` + `patch_engine_v19.py` from the prior v18 baseline. The self-contained browser observer uses a single dynamically selected camera mode, with the same pitch and world samples exposed to the overlay and names. This version is **not** the Node.js/SQLite Native shared engine.

This repository branch includes presentation-only source contracts:
- `src/presentation/mini/BallDoubleCadence.ts`: prior 27.5ms twice-rate batted-ball visual sampling.
- `src/presentation/mini/BroadcastScenePolicy.ts`: new pitch ball cadence constant, batter→runner observer selection, foul tap 2s/5s cut timing, no early fair cutoff contract, runner name color.
- `src/presentation/mini/BroadcastScenePolicy.test.ts`: unit assertions for these transitions.
- `src/presentation/mini/index.ts`: exports both modules.

The typed source **does not yet wire real physical world snapshots or a production Native closure ledger to the v19 browser application**; it is a reusable presentation contract for that later integration.
