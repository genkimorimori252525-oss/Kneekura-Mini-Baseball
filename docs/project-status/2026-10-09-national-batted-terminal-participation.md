## Connected owners

`confirmNationalActualLivePlayed` issues `NATIONAL_ACTUAL_LIVE_V1` only after the
original actual-live closure is officially applied. `confirmNationalFoulTerminalPlayed`
issues `NATIONAL_FOUL_TERMINAL_V1` only after the existing terminal owner completes
its post-play effects. Both replay the original Native National Match membership,
registration, eligibility, Person, fixture and participant bindings. An incoming
half's defenders or a next batter cannot become participants in the closed play.
Batted evidence retains its original BATTER_RUNNER role; terminal evidence retains
BATTER. Neither infers another running action.

Actual-live closure and post-play review now select the original National fixture
through the same small reader, without requiring a domestic season surrogate.
The domestic branch preserves its complete existing scheduled-game object and
proposal bytes. Incoming half-change defenders are independently checked against
original National membership by both actual-live and foul-terminal completion.

The two explicit National formats feed existing appearance adoption, owned
popularity and National ELITE_EXPOSURE initiation. Consumers authenticate the
receipt on their own Native connection. Adoption retains the original registration
snapshot, and development retains one opportunity per Career/game/Player. Caller
audience, personal appraisal, motif and policy remain explicit accepted inputs.
Later call-up replacement, later adoption and current roster state cannot rewrite
historical proof. No participation-only growth multiplier is introduced.

Existing legacy and domestic receipt identities and bytes remain unchanged.
Another format or another closure for an already recorded game/Player follows
the existing explicit collision rule. Exact retry and historical reads replay
original proof; fresh admission also requires the current completed Match.
Post-INSERT proof checking and ordinary rollback remain in the same transaction.
All National formats remain ineligible for domestic clinical REHAB fixtures.

National game with its actual registration, eligibility and Match origin. Its
first batter takes two pitches and makes an original bunt contact; existing
field, foul, official, TOTAL workload, scoring and completion owners produce the
terminal receipt. The completed activation admits the next original batter,
whose independent contact, ground/capture, motion, first-base rule, umpire,
communication, physical end and official closure produce the batted receipt.

close/reopen, exact retry, later eligibility change and same-game duplicate

inputs and supports the caller's original participants and accepted base center.
