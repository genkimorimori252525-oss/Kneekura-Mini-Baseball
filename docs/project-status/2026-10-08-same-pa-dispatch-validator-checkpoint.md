# Same-PA first-dispatch validator checkpoint

This checkpoint implements the bounded first stage of the approved dispatch inventory: exact Source shapes and numerical domains, a read-only census for the ten `pa_dispatch_v1_*` tables, the original batter plus nine defender roles, and the 32 required calibration bindings. It installs no schema, authenticates no durable dispatch owner and grants no physical admission.

The schema census accepts only all-absent storage or all ten exact tables with their exact columns and automatic indexes. Partial, extra, malformed, case-alias, view, trigger, foreign-index and temporary-shadow objects reject without repair. Canonical SQL keys cover action, calibration, consumer-set, episode, right, pitch, head, consumption and admission identities. The sole pitch revision is constrained to one.

The Source parser preserves full nominal owner references and separately typed effective responses. Batting value-domain validation is shared with the original batting-model owner without fabricating a modified v1 Source. Pitch calibration retains a nominal timing endpoint and a separate response-policy reference. The action keeps the explicit seed, mound reference, outing and ready time as well as delivery intent/physics and TAKE geometry. Those four context inputs were restored following independent Source review; their omission and invalid domains have a reached RED/GREEN witness. Owner-specific chronology and model authentication remain following work.

Role membership and order come from the original actor. The view supplies exact binding, Person, reserved-state and projected-state hashes. Missing calibration references are reported by player and route; cross-participant Source aliases, foreign roles and caller-selected skip/support fields reject. All 32 adapter bindings remain explicitly unsupported at this checkpoint. Consumer-action operation payloads are unexposed until their concrete Core adapter unions exist.
