# Accepted domestic calendar day dispatch

This is an area 9 connection under the [original nine-area checkpoint §5](2026-10-04-nonvisual-implementation-checkpoint.md#5-残る確定済み非デザイン計画). It consumes an explicitly accepted Career/day command through `dispatchDomesticCalendarDay`, exported by `DomesticSeasonRuntime`.

## Existing authorities and durable effects

- `SqliteDomesticScheduleStore.readCareerDay` reads registered Career seasons and the current accepted schedule revisions. It returns due regular fixtures and original market-trigger references. A World season without a dated archive, or an archive without its accepted event profile, remains explicit missing input.
- Due games use the existing fixture and Match writers. Rules and initial play identity remain explicit. A retained fixture authenticates its original Club journal revision, including after later same-day Club events. Partial fixture/Match writes can reopen and retry; a game moved to another day is rejected on the old command.
- Pending regular and foul-terminal World receipts are discovered from the existing outbox and retried through their original final, schedule, attendance and World owners. Required player-outcome delivery remains incomplete after World commit when its owner is absent. A pre-World request whose frozen projection conflicts with a later accepted schedule revision stays rejected and pending; the dispatcher does not rewrite its request.
- An explicit market task supplies its original trigger reference, source ID, expected recruitment revision and original decision day. The recruitment writer checks that association and decision day inside its Native transaction. Original decision day can precede the dispatch day. An optional, explicitly accepted free-agent contract is delivered with the actual persisted decision reference; its acceptance and Club event cannot be future-dated relative to dispatch. Legacy decisions without calendar admission retain their original bytes.
- An explicit roster task supplies the existing opportunity input and original Manager belief revision. Issuance uses the existing legal-candidate/Manager/Club/roster owners. An optional accepted execution uses the existing roster writer. Reopen and later belief history retain the original decision context, and changed original evidence is rejected.

Each item reports `APPLIED`, `MISSING_INPUT`, `INCOMPLETE`, or `REJECTED`, plus the available durable result. A failed contract or roster execution keeps its successfully accepted decision/opportunity visible. `outstanding` includes unsuccessful work and unassociated due market notices. No aggregate result means that the day is complete. A command retry repeats the accepted input; original commands are not reconstructed from calendar notifications or the success of a subset. Existing outbox receipts are independently discoverable.

A persisted Career current-day owner and automatic day advancement remain implementation work. `worldRevision` continues to identify accepted decisions, not elapsed time. Automatic advancement additionally needs the explicit completion/pause contract: which unresolved games, manual decisions, market notices and downstream obligations prevent advancing. No such aggregate rule is supplied by these calendar contracts. Producing lower-tier schedules, autonomous candidate/negotiation/opportunity inputs, or the physical actions needed to finish a prepared Match remains separate from this accepted-input dispatch. Lineups, bodies, actions and policy calibration are still owned by their existing accepted sources.

## Admission correction

The explicit Manager belief revision now enters `issueOpportunity`'s Native transaction and is retained in new issued receipts. Current admission checks that exact revision; historical reads replay its original prefix. Equal agent values at a later revision cannot replace the accepted provenance. Legacy calls omit the additive field and keep their existing receipt shape.

Accepted-day preparation passes a calendar-owner guard into both existing fixture and Match write transactions. It checks the day, accepted schedule and original Club venue before effects, after insertion and on retry. The calendar owner authenticates its database identity against the actual Native writer, so this guarded path requires calendar/World and Match on the same database. Existing preparation without an accepted day keeps its separate-database behavior. Writer-local changes are rejected within the transaction and roll back its writes.

`DomesticCalendarDayAdmission.test.ts` covers real accepted interleavings before both writes, writer-local calendar rollback at both insertion points, stale Manager admission with changed or equal agent values, original-revision retry/reopen, receipt-pin removal rollback and copied-database rejection.
