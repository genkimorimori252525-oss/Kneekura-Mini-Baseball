# Non-pitch physical variants and mixed practice exposure

This additive area-8 batch follows
[the original game-repetition connection](2026-10-09-non-pitch-game-repetitions.md)
and the approved learning contract in design document 53. It keeps accepted
personal relevance, exposure coefficients, health assessment and calibration
explicit. It adds no autonomous practice scheduler or ability formula.

## Physical repetition adapters

The prospective opportunity still names the original actor, Player, Person and
learning-episode revision. Admission must precede both ordinary physical pitch
work and any reserved same-PA enrollment/work. Historical reads keep that exact
original frame after later Match progression. The original game/play/Player
reservation remains exclusive across exercise labels.

The existing ordinary actual-live completion also accepts an authenticated
episode-field root, retaining its original episode binding reference. Unbound
ordinary evidence retains its previous JSON bytes and proof hash.

A reserved completion can name the existing same-PA terminal endpoint. This
adapter requires the original completed fair-catch endpoint, the exact sealed
field generation, the official terminal transition/release and all original
TOTAL workload effects. It derives:

- Batting contact from the original executed swing commitment and resolution.
- Defender glove contact from the executed field contact history.
- Running motion from a positive-duration original offensive body segment with
  actual nonzero horizontal velocity or acceleration.

Occupied original membership uses the existing participant owner. An occupied
runner holding still produces no running repetition. Membership, a planned
curve, an outcome label or a catcher role cannot stand in for executed motion.
The currently owned occupied fair-catch terminal restricts runners to its
supported stationary case; moving occupied completion still needs a compatible
terminal endpoint producer. Other reserved outcome families remain unsupported.

Both completion families bind fatigue to the original MATCH workload BEFORE.
Repetition adoption never charges a second workload or automatically changes an
ability. Actual contact is separate from the accepted relevance assessment.

## Mixed exposure

`openSqliteDevelopmentPracticeExposureStore` accepts an explicit Source naming
one exact consolidated episode revision, an exposure policy/prior, calibrated
pitch coefficients and their assessment/calibration provenance. It assembles
every original practice event in episode order:

- Pitch events use the registered original pitch-practice owner. The consumed
  attempt, original PRACTICE receipt, learning application, fatigue BEFORE and
  assessed health are authenticated on the exposure owner's Native snapshot.
- Non-pitch events use the fixed original repetition decoder on that same
  snapshot, including its accepted assessment, physical proof and learning
  application.

Missing, extra, repeated, foreign or unsupported repetition evidence fails.
Caller pitch coefficients cannot replace the frozen non-pitch factors. The
existing Core `assessDevelopmentPracticeExposure` checks complete event coverage
and performs its unchanged calculation. The persisted Source, original episode,
ordered proof hashes, bundle and assessment replay on read/retry/reopen; the
writer rechecks them after INSERT before commit.

This exposure record is evidence, not a new learning admission or source-change
receipt. It does not relax the existing pitch timing learner's requirement for
its original standardized NORMAL/QUICK measurement pairs.

## Remaining production connections

These are code/ownership gaps, not requests to invent numerical defaults:

- Batting's explicit replacement capability and original model are now bound to
  consolidated exposure by the [accepted capability history
  connection](2026-10-10-accepted-batting-capability-history.md). Generic model
  readers replay that provenance while retaining the existing dated selection.
- Fielding, runner decision/motion and defender locomotion owners currently
  enforce one immutable Player baseline. They need validated append-only
  adaptation histories and historical selectors before explicit changed models
  can reach execution without rewriting earlier bodies/actions.
- Standalone non-pitch drills need a prospective action/body producer, actual
  consumed completion and original PRACTICE workload ownership. Match repetition
  evidence cannot be relabeled as a standalone drill.
- Other reserved terminal families and moving occupied-runner completion need
  their corresponding original endpoint/workload adapter or producer.

Autonomous relevance, appraisal, drill selection and numerical capability or
physiological adaptation remain explicit accepted inputs until their production
assessment/calibration models are defined. Pitch timing measurements do not
define batting timing or fielding/running ability changes.

## Verification boundary

Small Native author tests cover real consumed pitch practice with the original
development/workload owners, substituted Match completion evidence, mixed
exposure replay and trigger rollback. Separate adapter tests substitute the
physical/terminal owners and check their exact contracts, unsupported controls
and ordinary byte preservation. Original actor admission has a small genuine
registration fixture. None of these tests qualifies a full reserved/occupied
physical game; the combined compiler/review and genuine composition remain the
integration owner's verification step.
