# Occupied runner motion and tag-up evidence connection

This bounded connection executes one explicitly accepted finite straight advance for an original occupied runner. It reuses the runner hold's authenticated Player/Person, five-part body and runner decision-motion model, and the existing `SamePlateAppearanceRunnerControllerMotion` physical adapter. It adds no physiology values or autonomous runner decision.

## Source and execution ownership

- `occupied_runner_motion_v1` names the original hold, reserved participant, straight route, issued advance, finite end and provenance. The first accepted physical consumer starts at an exact current cut of the original stationary body.
- Each continuation pins the latest actual motion for that runner and retains the same issued command/controller. Its member must match the current workload projection; changing that projection does not replace the original reserved identity or controller.
- Reaction, acceleration and speed-limit pieces remain separate physical cuts. Foreign command coverage and original body geometry are preserved. This connection stays within the original hold's finite coverage.
- The live census retains adoption, future reaction, current analytic-piece coverage and controller-end work. The actual motion primitives enter the existing rule-system retirement inventory.
- Actual contact histories for home, first, second and third are projected from executed five-part segments. Their exact elapsed times and quantized ticks are retained. The physical history producer does not declare legal occupancy, SAFE/OUT, tag-up compliance or PlayEnd.
- The new history/census properties appear only on the new motion path, preserving existing empty and stationary projections. A received stationary hold cannot replace an adopted advance, even during reaction. The stationary occupied catch finalizer cannot consume that moving controller.

## Authenticated tag-up evidence

The Native paired field-rule reader now adds `occupiedRunnerTagUp` only for the new motion path. It reads the original Match, holds and physical prefix in the same existing read phase. Empty and stationary archives keep their previous projection shape.

`deriveSamePaOccupiedRunnerTagUp` checks exact occupied membership, original Player/Person/body/base/enrollment, finite coverage and continuous actual history. It rederives each origin-base history, uses `createRunnerBaseFactsFromBallWorldHistory`, and pins the original first-fielder contact's field reference, physical moment and fact. Actual airborne secured catch evidence must agree with that original contact.

The latest actual departure and its actual subsequent origin-base retouch feed `evaluateBallWorldTagUpCompliance`; earlier excursions stay in the full history. The new bounded Core adapter reuses `evaluateTagUpCompliance` wherever its tick result faithfully represents the physical ordering, leaving the old API unchanged. Early departure remains `appealable_early_departure`. An actual post-first-touch retouch restores compliance. No departure is manufactured from an observed horizon, and an unconfirmed catch cannot establish tag-up compliance.

Known exact elapsed ordering survives quantization, including a real early departure whose tick equals first-fielder touch. Exact equal moments retain the existing Core equality contract. An actual origin-base contact interval spanning first-fielder touch establishes `contact_at_first_fielder_touch` compliance without inserting a synthetic retouch or departure. The result retains exact first-touch, departure, retouch and legal-advance times alongside the unchanged physical facts. Missing original contact or unconfirmed/ambiguous first-fielder physical evidence stays pending.

The initial missing-adapter case failed as expected, then passed. The bounded motion/evidence selection passed 43 cases in 6.17 seconds. After the exact-history extension, the final affected selection passed 29 cases in 5.59 seconds: 21 occupied evidence cases and all eight existing tick-only compliance/vertical-slice cases. Coverage includes actual departure/retouch, continuous contact at first touch, missing initial contact, original source linkage, multiple occupied identities, large-origin clocks, exact equality, quantized ordering and malformed ownership. Two earlier ambiguity fixtures initially crossed a quantizer boundary; their geometry was corrected to produce distinct moments within one tick. These were fixture corrections, not product-RED evidence.

## Remaining code and policy boundaries

The original stationary fair-catch scoring and closure owners still reject moving history. The new tag-up sidecar is legal evidence, not an official ruling or physical end. The live census continues to retain all existing reaction, observation, controller and custody work.

Existing `orchestrateTagUpAppealAttempt` / `resolveTagUpAppeal` can consume an actual defensive appeal and the owned RuleProfile window. No accepted defensive appeal action exists in this connection, so `original_defensive_appeal_action_required` remains pending. The timeline variant additionally requires a real completed physical timeline.

Remaining implementation is an authenticated actual appeal-attempt consumer and moving-runner scoring/official-window integration. Known exact ordering and first-touch-spanning contact intervals are now interpreted; genuinely missing or ambiguous physical evidence must remain unresolved until its original owner supplies it. The separate legacy `SqliteBattedWorldExecutionStore` base-history route registers only the batter and defenders and cannot substitute as occupied-runner authority.

No new policy was needed for original identity mapping or the existing unambiguous tag-up contract. Autonomous route choice, reaction to a caught call, a return/turn command, and a phase-ending hold policy remain outside this connection. Synthetic return curves in the bounded evidence tests exercise existing physical history kernels and do not issue a production return policy.
