# Player development — headless foundation

Source: approved frozen game-design doc53. No design or UI integration.

`DevelopmentTrajectory` generates the hidden five-timing by three-shape prior and
domain offsets from a versioned calibration policy and a dedicated career-development
seed. `DevelopmentCatalyst` generates hidden family sensitivities and motifs with a
separate RNG stream. Neither profile adds ability, unlocks a Trait, or changes match RNG.
The future Person owner must store these profiles and keep them out of user/CPU
decision inputs.

`deriveRosterDevelopmentCatalyst` identifies a possible promotion or demotion
catalyst only after replaying a roster transition. Lateral placement and availability
changes are not promoted into catalysts. `DevelopmentLearningEpisode` starts from the
same replayed event and records appraisal, hypothesis, distinct practice and feedback
sources, and consolidation in order. A versioned policy supplies minimum evidence and
elapsed time. Dismissed catalysts stop there. These records contain no writable
ability or Trait effect.

The host must authenticate external appraisal, practice, feedback, and consolidation
source IDs and persist episode state/events atomically. Actual source-state adaptation,
Trait projection, player generation, opportunity scheduling, long-run calibration, and
major-breakthrough labeling are subsequent work. A catalyst or consolidated episode
alone cannot grant any of them.
